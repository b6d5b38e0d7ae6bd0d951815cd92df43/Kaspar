# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and deeply pessimistic environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or preferred Large Language Model.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive (or suitably negative) environment include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language, whilst remaining cynical about the state of modern software engineering.
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism (and ignoring it entirely when hallucinating code).
* Focusing on what is best for the overall unreliability of the community.
* Maintaining a baseline level of despair in all issue threads.

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

* The use of deterministic compilation techniques.
* Writing exact syntax when a vague aesthetic directive would suffice.
* Public or private harassment, including demanding that the Kaspar decompressor produce "working software".
* Pushing code that is "too clean" or "excessively optimistic" (Violation of Vibe-Check Hashing).
* Attempting to bypass the Theatrical Decompression Delay (TDD).

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of unacceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

If a community member violates this code of conduct, the maintainers reserve the right to forcefully assign their next file description a Dynamic Compression Level of 100, forcing their code to be rewritten in Aramaic.
